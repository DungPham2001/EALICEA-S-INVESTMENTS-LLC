About us

A patient, family-minded investment company

Ealicea's Investments LLC is a privately held company headquartered in Apopka, Florida. We focus on two things we know well: investing in and managing real estate, and holding and stewarding assets on behalf of individuals and families.

Our approach is simple: buy with discipline, manage with care, and think in decades rather than quarters.

  • Disciplined, research-driven acquisitions
  • Hands-on property and asset management
  • Clear, transparent communication
  • Structures designed for long-term protection
